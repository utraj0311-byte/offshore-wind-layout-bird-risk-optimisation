## Results text (ready to edit for thesis)

The 72-sector Metric 4b optimisation yielded 36 mutually non-dominated layouts. The highest-AEP endpoint produced 828.6682 GWh/year and the lowest-risk endpoint 813.8397 GWh/year at 5-degree wind-direction resolution. The lowest-risk layout therefore sacrificed 14.8286 GWh/year (1.79%) relative to the highest-AEP layout. Both endpoints met the 1-degree AEP convergence criterion, with deviations of 0.01820% and 0.01165%, respectively.

On the optimisation sample of 10,000 birds per layout, $Q_{4\mathrm{b}}$ was 0.00192393 for highest-AEP and 0.00073657 for lowest-risk. The independently evaluated values on a shared widened entry corridor (15 batches of 100,000 birds per layout) were 0.00196853 and 0.00084920, respectively. The paired difference (lowest-risk minus highest-AEP) was -0.00111933 with a 95% confidence interval [-0.00113468, -0.00110398]. This represents 56.86% fewer independently evaluated expected rotor transits per entering bird at the lowest-risk endpoint under the specified behavioural assumptions.

The highest-AEP layout extended 89.798 m beyond the original rotor-margin corridor, motivating a common 8577.080 m corridor for independent evaluation. For direct visual comparison with optimisation-sample values, the independent-evaluation scores were width-normalised to the original 8485.281 m corridor; the adjusted values are 0.00198983 and 0.00085839. They exceeded their respective optimisation-sample values by 3.43% and 16.54%. Width-normalisation should not be interpreted as a new independent evaluation within the narrower corridor.

The no-response control ($p=0$) produced practically identical means (0.00547773 and 0.00547773), with a paired difference whose 95% interval includes zero. Thus, when all rotor zones are covered, expected total rotor transits of straight-flying birds are approximately layout-independent. At $p=0.6$, the layout difference emerges, consistent with the interaction between turbine arrangement and the prescribed meso-response rule. The lowest-risk layout had five closely aligned turbine pairs along the bird-flight axis compared with none in the highest-AEP layout. The fixed-height $z=150$ m experiment also retained a reduction at $p=0.6$ (paired difference -0.076087), whereas at $p=0$ the difference was indistinguishable from zero.

The new endpoint trade-off survived independent evaluation; therefore the earlier observation that the apparent rotor-transit trade-off disappeared does not repeat for these two selected endpoints. However, only two of the 36 non-dominated layouts received this independent evaluation, and $Q_{4\mathrm{b}}$ measures expected rotor-disc transits rather than blade strikes or collision mortality. The Monte Carlo intervals quantify sampling uncertainty for the frozen model and chosen layouts, not uncertainty in the biological response assumptions.
