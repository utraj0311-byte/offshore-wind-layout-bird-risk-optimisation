### 3b v5 prior Pareto seeds
The 60 previously non-dominated Metric 3b initial layouts were taken from 61 v5 **development-validation** archive layouts, seed 47/p160/g120 (itself consolidating v2/v3/v4), and **not** from the final completed 12-sector seed-48 run. The other starting layouts are five designed exact layouts, 75 jitters **of those designed layouts only**, and 20 random feasible layouts.

### Origin of bundled plot PDFs
The six thesis vector PDFs were copied unchanged from `Section_3_3_Overleaf_ready.zip`, generated by local postprocessing scripts from original saved result archives. No figures in this package are regenerated from inferred coordinates.

### Initial population/reproduction caveat
Metric 3a: new seed-42 300-generation trajectory diverged from historical seed-42 generation 2. Current-environment repeated 120-gen replays matched each other and the first 120 generations of new 300-gen trajectory. Historical package versions were not recorded, so the cause cannot be proven. The 300-gen runs are **separate** tests, not verified continuation.
